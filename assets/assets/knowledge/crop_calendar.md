# Crop Calendar and Seasonal Advisory (North India)

## Kharif Season (June to October)
Kharif crops are sown with the onset of the monsoon and harvested in autumn. Recommended crops for this farm: Paddy (rice), Soybean, Maize, and Cotton. Sow paddy nursery in mid-June and transplant after 21 to 25 days. Soybean should be sown by the first week of July for best yields.

## Rabi Season (November to April)
Rabi crops are sown in winter. Recommended crops: Wheat, Gram (chana), Mustard, and Barley. The optimal wheat sowing window for Ghazipur is November 1 to November 20. Late sowing after November 25 reduces yield by roughly 1.5 percent per day of delay.

## Zaid Season (April to June)
Short summer season between Rabi and Kharif. Suitable crops: Cucumber, Watermelon, Muskmelon, and fodder maize. Irrigation is critical because of high evapotranspiration; irrigate every 4 to 6 days.

## Crop Rotation Advice
After harvesting a cereal like wheat or paddy, rotate to a legume such as gram, moong, or soybean. Legumes fix atmospheric nitrogen and restore soil fertility, reducing the urea requirement for the next cereal crop.
