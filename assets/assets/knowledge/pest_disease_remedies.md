# Common Pests, Diseases and Organic Remedies

## Aphids (Mahu / Chepa)
Small soft-bodied insects on the undersides of leaves and on tender shoots. They cause curling and yellowing. Organic control: spray Neem oil at 1500 PPM, 5 ml per litre of water, in the evening. Chemical option only if infestation exceeds 15 percent: Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.5 ml per litre.

## Yellow Rust in Wheat
Yellow to orange powdery stripes along the leaf veins, common in cool humid weather. Remove and destroy heavily infected leaves. Spray Propiconazole 25% EC at 1 ml per litre at first appearance. Resistant varieties like HD-3086 reduce risk.

## Yellowing Leaves — Diagnosis Guide
If OLDER lower leaves yellow first and the plant is generally pale, it is most likely nitrogen deficiency — apply a top dressing of urea. If YOUNGER upper leaves yellow between the veins, it points to iron or zinc deficiency — apply a foliar micronutrient spray. If yellowing is patchy with wilting, check for waterlogging or root disease.

## Organic Soil Boosters
Neem cake at 100 kg per acre improves soil health and deters nematodes. Compost tea applied as a soil drench boosts microbial activity. Vermicompost at 2 tonnes per acre is excellent before sowing Rabi crops.
