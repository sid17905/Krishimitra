# Wheat Fertilizer & Nutrient Schedule (Ghazipur, UP)

## Basal Dose at Sowing
Apply DAP at 55 kg per acre plus Muriate of Potash (MOP) at 20 kg per acre at sowing time. Mix Zinc Sulphate at 10 kg per acre if the soil test shows zinc deficiency, which is common in this region's alluvial soils.

## First Top Dressing (CRI Stage, 21 to 25 days)
Apply Urea at 45 kg per acre at the Crown Root Initiation stage. This is the single most critical irrigation and nitrogen window for wheat. Ensure soil moisture is between 40 and 70 percent before application.

## Second Top Dressing (Late Tillering, 45 days)
Apply a second dose of Urea at 45 kg per acre. If leaves are pale green or yellowing (nitrogen deficiency), this dose corrects it. Yellowing of lower/older leaves first usually indicates nitrogen deficiency, not disease.

## Local Soil Note
The farmer's Block A and Block B soils typically read Nitrogen around 40 to 45 mg/kg (moderate), Phosphorus 18 to 22 mg/kg, and Potassium 30 to 35 mg/kg. pH sits near 6.8 to 7.2 which is ideal for wheat.
